SPEAKER 1 · ANDREW
THE HOOK · WHY THIS BOOK, WHY NOW

Tariffs, a yen unwind, a crypto winter

Five very different markets in five years. Each paid one kind of strategy and punished another. Here is which sleeve did what.
2021–22 · Crypto winter
Bitcoin fell 74% from its peak. Anyone who just held it took the whole loss.
The wheel: max drawdown −28%, against −74% for the coin; 2022 −16.6% vs −64.2%.
2022 · The rate shock
Stocks and bonds fell together, so the usual hedge did not work.
Short IDR +8.6% as the dollar rallied. E1 −2.2%, dip sat in cash (−1.2%). Book: −7.3% vs S&P 500 −19.4%.
Aug 2024 · The yen unwind
Three days of forced selling worldwide when the yen carry trade unwound.
The dip sleeve bought it (+5.7%); wheel −3.4%, short IDR flat (dollar filter off). Book: −0.6%.
Apr 2025 · The Trump tariff shock
Tariffs announced, paused and announced again within weeks.
The dip sleeve bought the tariff dip (+13.7% over 1–11 Apr); wheel +4.6%. Book: +3.6%.
Oct 2026 · The live window
1 to 30 Oct: S&P 500 −0.9%, Bitcoin −6.9%, chip sell-off mid-month.
Wheel +5.3%, short IDR +1.6%, E1 −0.5%, dip −9.3%. Book: +1.42% (+18.7% annualised).
No one strategy did well in all five. The book is built so that one piece of news cannot hurt every sleeve at the same time.

Design
  • Bet the rupiah weakens: long USD/IDR at 1× through 1-month forwards, rolled monthly

  • Every Friday close: a one-page report per sleeve and for the book

  • Lowest point in October: −0.1%, on 1 Oct
Reporting

CONCLUSION

What we built, and what would change our minds

1 · Four sources of return, none of them dominant• Correlations −0.09 to +0.15; book equity beta +0.16
• Two sleeves lose when markets fall; measured over 291 weeks
2 · The book holds when its parts fall• Sharpe 1.39 vs 0.23 to 1.46 for the sleeves
• 2022: −7.3% vs −19.4% for the S&P 500; Oct 2026: +1.42% while dip lost 9.3%
• −14.9% drawdown in the Mar 2025 HYPE crash: the wheel is return and risk
3 · Seven designs dropped, all listed in A3• A regime-timed wheel, a commodity-stock sleeve with its two filters, and a trend-futures sleeve failed or were replaced
• Trend filters on the rupiah and a one-coin wheel rule were rejected
4 · Sized by what can go wrong, fixed in advance• The wheel has the worst tail (A7), so the brake is set before it is needed
What would change our minds: a sleeve breaking its kill rule live. We stop it and report it.

APPENDIX A1 · SPECIFICATIONS
Purpose: The exact rules of every sleeve, fixed in advance, so nothing can be changed quietly

Frozen specification sheet

SleeveSpecification
E13 PCA factors on a 60-day window. Open a trade when the s-score passes ±1.5, close when it comes back inside ∓0.5, stop if |s| passes 3.5. Only names whose residual half-life is 30 days or less. 34 names (9 SGX, 25 US). 10 bps charged on every change in position weight (entries, exits, daily rebalancing).
WheelEvery Friday, sell a 1-week option at 25 delta: a put when holding no coin, a call when holding it. Cash fully covers the put and earns the T-bill rate; premium is cut by 5%. BTC only to 3 Jan 2025, priced at Deribit's DVOL; from then 70% HYPE / 30% BTC, rebalanced each quarter, HYPE priced at its 30-day realised vol. If a coin closes a Friday below its own 200-day average, its leg runs the next week at half size (BTC) or sits in T-bills (HYPE), and goes back to full size once the coin closes above.
Coin screenHow we pick the wheel's second coin: the protocol spends its own revenue buying the token back; no large VC or insider supply waiting to unlock; real fee-paying usage; enough spot liquidity to trade every week. HYPE passes (fees fund buybacks). BTC has no protocol revenue, so it stays as the liquid core (A9).
Short IDRLong USD/IDR at 1× through rolling 1-month forwards, held only while the dollar index's 21-day return is positive (checked at each close, applied the next day). Returns in USD. Carry of 3.0% a year (the assumed IDR–USD rate gap) while on, plus 3 bps per roll and 5 bps per switch. No stop apart from the kill rule.
DipNasdaq-100 (101 current names), only those above their 200-day average. Signal: a 1-day drop of more than 3% with ATR(5)/close above 3%. Next day, a limit buy at the signal close minus 0.9×ATR(5); candidates ranked by ATR(5)/close. At most 10 positions, each a tenth of the prior-close equity (reconstructed). Exit at +0.5×ATR, on a close above the prior day's high, or on day 11. No stop loss. IBKR tiered commissions.
Book20/15/15/50 (E1/dip/short IDR/wheel), rebalanced each quarter. Brake: halve every position at a −5% book drawdown, stop at −8%.
